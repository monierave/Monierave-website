Privacy Policy.

How MonieRave handles information when you visit our website or use our services.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MonieRave, a product operated and developed by Entzopedia Technologies Limited (“MonieRave”, “we”, “us” or “our”), may collect, use, disclose and protect information when you visit our website, create an account, or use our services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the services you use, information may include your name, email address, phone number, account and profile details, identity-verification information, transaction and payment records, support messages, device and browser information, log data, and security-related information. We collect information you provide, information generated by your use of the service, and information received from service providers where permitted by law.

2. How we use information

We may use information to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; provide requested features; process and record transactions where available; prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access; provide customer support; maintain and secure our systems; comply with legal obligations; and communicate important service, security or policy updates.

3. Legal grounds and consent

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on grounds such as providing a service you request, complying with legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or your consent. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to legal limitations.

4. Sharing information

We may share information with infrastructure, authentication, payment, identity-verification, analytics, communications, fraud-prevention and customer-support providers when needed to operate the service; with professional advisers; with regulators or law enforcement when legally required; or in connection with a corporate restructuring. We do not state that every listed service is currently active. We do not sell personal information as a business model.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including security, dispute resolution, accounting and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain certain records where law permits or requires.

7. Cookies and device data

Our website or service may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for security, session management and functionality. If analytics or optional tracking technologies are introduced, we will provide notices or choices where required by law.

8. Children

MonieRave is not intended for anyone who cannot legally enter into the relevant service agreement. Do not create an account or provide personal information if you are not eligible under applicable law and our service requirements.

9. International processing and policy changes

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, subject to applicable safeguards. We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The revised date will be shown on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@monierave.com. MonieRave is operated and developed by Entzopedia Technologies Limited.

This page is a general policy draft and should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions and financial services MonieRave actually operates in before launch.
