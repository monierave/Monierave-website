GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is Monierave operating?

Yes. Visit app.monierave.com to create an account or sign in. The services available to you are shown inside your account.

Which currency is available?

Nigerian naira (NGN) is currently available. USD, EUR and GBP wallet options are coming soon and are not yet available for transactions.

Can I make a transaction on this website?

No. This is Monierave's public information website. For account access or help with a service, contact support@monierave.com.

Are the phone screens live account screenshots?

No. They are illustrative product previews and do not show a live account or current transaction history.

Who operates Monierave?

Monierave is operated and developed by Entzopedia Technologies Limited.

How can I contact support?

Email support@monierave.com. Never send your password, PIN, or one-time code by email.

Our support team is here to help.
